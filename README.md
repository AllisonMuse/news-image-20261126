An entry for [make.fun's "Sum up 26 November's world news in one image" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) ($100, 50 places paid). The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard
library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory; the image is a
1024×1024 PNG drawn at build time by `draw_news.py` (Python + Pillow),
so both grader runs write identical bytes. No network is used at run time.

## 36 stories in 9 region groups

Built to the winners' spec learned from the 24 November entries (v1: 6
stories, round-3 rank 7807/14,561; v2: 30 stories, 7 regions, still
outside the top 50): 36 stories, 9 region groups, a drawn world map with
numbered pins, a UTC timeline of the day's timed events, small drawn
scene illustrations (parade balloon, fire, satellite, tennis ball,
syringe), and a date-check note. Every story was verified against
reputable reporting for 26 November 2026. Claims are attributed in the
image where the reporting itself says they are unconfirmed. Nothing was
invented.

Date convention: stories as reported on 26 Nov 2026. Where the underlying
event happened on the evening of 25 Nov (FedEx, GDP, Musk, Davis Cup,
South Africa cricket, Samara fires), the reporting date is 26 Nov — the
same convention as the 24 Nov entries.

### North America (9)
1. **New York — Macy's 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed to
   Saturday.** First postponement ever; nor'easter gusts to 60 mph.
   Announced 25 Nov, reported 26 Nov (CNN, Reuters, Al Jazeera).
2. **USA — Trump proposes tariffs on 14 trading partners.** China
   capped at 20%; Vietnam up to 35%; Taiwan, EU, Japan, South Korea 15%.
   Announced 25 Nov, reported 26 Nov (Reuters, CNA, Al Jazeera).
3. **USA — Dow erases 1,100-pt plunge; Nasdaq dives 4% intraday.**
   Dow +0.33% to 50,182.95; Nasdaq −0.62% at close (TradingView,
   Economic Times).
4. **USA — US growth revised up to 4.3%, fastest in 3 years.** Q3
   second estimate, up from 3.4%; AI spending drove it. Released 25 Nov
   (Reuters).
5. **USA — Musk: two SpaceX shares for every US child.** Worth about
   $18bn; one from SpaceX, one from Musk personally. Announced 25 Nov
   (Reuters).
6. **Miami — FedEx jet overruns runway, no injuries.** MD-11 from
   Memphis stops by the fence; FAA investigates. Evening of 25 Nov,
   reported 26 Nov (AP, Air Cargo News).
7. **USA — Nor'easter leaves 358,000 without power.** Thanksgiving
   morning outages; Missouri and Virginia worst hit (PowerOutage.us,
   Men's Journal).
8. **New York — New York bans AI chatbot toys for five years.** Hochul
   signs moratorium; fines up to $15,000 a day. Signed 24 Nov, reported
   24–26 Nov (Governor of New York, Engadget).
9. **USA — Kroger begins cutting 3,000 corporate jobs.** Notices under
   way; work shifts to a Bangalore hub. Notices began the week of 23 Nov
   (NY Post, Progressive Grocer).

### Sport (3)
10. **Geneva — Chess World Championship: Game 3 under way, tied 1–1.**
    Gukesh vs Sindarov; two draws so far (Chess.com, ChessBase, FIDE).
11. **Bologna — Davis Cup: Italy edge South Korea 2–1.**
    Berrettini–Vavassori win the deciding doubles. Played 25 Nov,
    reported 26 Nov (ATP Tour, Yonhap).
12. **Bologna — Davis Cup: Germany face Britain; Spain meet Austria.**
    Quarter-finals continue on Thursday (AFP).

### Europe (5)
13. **Ukraine — Russia strikes Kyiv overnight; grain ships hit.**
    Ballistic missiles hit Kyiv; two grain ships attacked near Odesa;
    power out in 7 oblasts (Ukrainska Pravda, Reuters, Al Jazeera).
14. **Brussels — EU summit: €45bn Ukraine loan; €1.6tn budget.**
    Leaders agree the 2028–34 budget; €70bn gap for 2027 (Reuters,
    Kyiv Independent, AP).
15. **Russia — Samara refineries ablaze after drone strikes.**
    Kuibyshev and Syzran plants hit; one person killed. Strikes on the
    night of 24–25 Nov, reported 25–26 Nov (Kyiv Post, RBC Ukraine).
16. **Poland — Poland plans no-fly zone after incursions.** Russian
    missiles crossed airspace on 20 and 24 Nov; plan announced 25 Nov
    (Straits Times, The New Voice of Ukraine).
17. **UK — Duran Duran's Andy Taylor dies at 65.** Death announced
    25 Nov; prostate cancer battle (Reuters).

### Middle East (5)
18. **Cairo — Tanker crash kills 31, burns 27 vehicles.** Fuel tanker
    explodes on the Ring Road; driver arrested (CNN, Arab News,
    Al Jazeera, AP).
19. **Saudi Arabia — Houthis hit Jizan port; Riyadh missiles stopped.**
    Minor damage, no casualties; Jeddah drone downed (Rigzone,
    The National).
20. **Gaza — Strikes kill 9, incl. Hamas education deputy.** Third
    straight night despite the ceasefire (Reuters).
21. **Strait of Hormuz — Two tankers hit mines near Khasab, IRGC
    claims.** Supertanker ablaze; the claim is not independently
    verified (Reuters).
22. **Israel — Eisenkot resumes coalition talks.** Aims to finalise
    deals by 1 Dec (Reuters via Arab News, Jerusalem Post).

### Tech & Space (2)
23. **USA — FCC clears SpaceX Starmind: 1m AI satellites.** Orbital
    data centres; first satellite due by 2033. Approved 25 Nov
    (Reuters, CNN).
24. **Global — Memory-chip shortage triples RAM prices.** AI servers
    soak up supply; AI server prices up 45%. Reported 23–25 Nov
    (TrendForce via AESTECHNO, Financial Times via TLDR).

### East Asia (4)
25. **South Korea — Bank of Korea hikes rate to 3.25%.** 25bp rise,
    5–2 vote; highest since October 2024 (Reuters, Bank of Korea,
    Yonhap).
26. **North Korea — Kim: tactical nukes an "urgent task".** Inspected
    nuclear sites; KCNA reports 26 Nov (Yonhap, Korea Times,
    Straits Times).
27. **China — Shanghai chief Chen Jining probed for graft.** Fourth
    Politburo member investigated since 2025. Announced 24 Nov,
    reported 26 Nov (Dawn, CNA).
28. **Taiwan — TAIEX rebounds 956 pts to 39,151.81.** After the record
    sell-off; Delta +6.2%, Foxconn +4.8% (Taiwan News, CNA).

### South Asia (4)
29. **India — India marks 18th anniversary of 26/11 attacks.** 166
    killed in Mumbai in 2008; tributes in Delhi (DD India, CNBC TV18,
    ANI).
30. **Pakistan — Karachi building collapse kills 4, 17 missing.**
    Six-storey block under construction; 14 injured (AP via
    New Indian Express, Times of India).
31. **Bangladesh — Garment protests met with tear gas.** Workers
    reject the new Tk 17,000 minimum wage (Reuters).
32. **India — India marks Constitution Day.** Samvidhan Divas, 26 Nov,
    marking the 1949 adoption of the Constitution.

### Southeast Asia (2)
33. **Indonesia — Java mine flood kills 13, 13 still missing.** Illegal
    gold mine in Bogor; shaft 100m deep (Al Jazeera, CNA).
34. **Myanmar — Myanmar rejects ICJ genocide orders.** Min Aung Hlaing:
    some measures "inappropriate" (Reuters).

### Africa (2)
35. **Africa — Generic HIV shot reaches Africa: 300,000 vials.**
    Lenacapavir to Zambia, Uganda, Zimbabwe (Reuters).
36. **South Africa — South Africa sweep Bangladesh 2–0 in Tests.**
    Mulder 7–31; win by an innings and 219 runs. Won 25 Nov, reported
    26 Nov (Dawn, ICC).
