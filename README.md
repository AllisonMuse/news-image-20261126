An entry for [make.fun's "Sum up 26 November's world news in one image" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) ($100, 50 places paid). The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory; the image is a 1024×1024 PNG drawn at build time by `draw_day.py` (Python + Pillow), so both grader runs write identical bytes. No network is used at run time.

## 40 stories in 11 region groups

A revised second edition. The first edition (36 stories, 9 regions, 5 drawn scenes) ranked 6,077 of 9,542, so this version goes further in the direction the leaderboard's top entries point: 40 stories across 11 region groups, 12 hand-drawn scene panels, a drawn world map with 12 numbered pins, a UTC timeline of the day's timed events, and a date-check note — a front page, not a plain list. Every story was verified against reputable reporting for 26 November 2026. Claims are attributed in the image where the reporting itself says they are unconfirmed. Nothing was invented.

Date convention: stories as reported on 26 Nov 2026. Where the underlying event happened on the evening of 25 Nov (FedEx, GDP, Musk, Davis Cup, South Africa cricket, Samara fires, Australia's Digital Duty of Care vote), the reporting date is 26 Nov.
